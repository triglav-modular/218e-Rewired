2.0 (2026-08-31)
- Step sequencer: 64 steps, recorded and played from the preset pads, with rests, ties and 303-style slides.
- External clock divide: with a clock patched in, the arpeggiator rate knob divides it, /1 to /8.
- Presets and the sequence survive power-off.
- Each preset knob can be assigned individually: note orders, swing, a bank of step patterns, the factory octave switch, or left factory.
- Preset voltages are set by holding a pad and turning the knob above it.
- Alternate tunings accept Scala keyboard maps, so a scale can have any number of degrees and repeat at something other than the octave. The octave controls step that period.
- Leaving the arpeggiator’s latch no longer stops notes still held by hand.

1.2 (2026-08-25)
- Improved pressure-based portamento behavior.
- Held-key state is cleared on the first boot after flashing, so a stuck gate no longer survives an update.

1.1 (2026-08-21)

- The macOS flasher is a signed, notarised app.

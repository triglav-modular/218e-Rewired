2.3 (2026-09-04)
- Any preset knob can now be set to None, which leaves it as a preset voltage.
- The add-to-pitch preset voltage can now be quantized to the selected tuning.
- The portamento in banana jack can now transpose the keyboard by whole degrees of the selected tuning. This feature also allows the keyboard to act as a basic pitch quantizer.
- Knob 2 can now be set to quantized randomness: beats drop out as the knob goes up, thinning the arpeggio, and past halfway notes appear on the half of the beat and fill it back in, never anywhere finer.
- Hold pads 2 and 3 together to change if “add to pitch” is applied before or after a note is entered in latch mode. The default state is “before”.
- Holding pad 4 for the sequencer, or pads 2 and 3 for the latch, now restore the original octave.

2.2 (2026-09-02)
- Fixed a portamento bug.
- The pitch offset can now be disabled.

2.1 (2026-09-02)
- Sequencer notes recorded below the initial octave now keep their MIDI notes after a power cycle.
- A tie at the end of a loop now holds its note to the next step instead of cutting it short.
- The clock divider’s gate is now half the step at every division.
- The strip LEDs now provide feedback when entering a tie or a rest.

2.0 (2026-08-31)
- Step sequencer: 64 steps, recorded and played from the preset pads.
- External clock divider: the arpeggiator rate knob now divides a patched clock from /1 to /8.
- Preset knobs can now be assigned individually.
- Preset voltages are now set by holding a pad and turning the knob above it.
- Alternate tunings now accept Scala keyboard maps, so a scale can have any number of degrees and repeat at something other than the octave.
- Leaving the arpeggiator’s latch no longer stops notes still held by hand.

1.2 (2026-08-25)
- Improved pressure-based portamento behavior.
- Held-key state is cleared on the first boot after flashing, so a stuck gate no longer survives an update.

1.1 (2026-08-21)
- trn and remote enable stay factory when nothing has taken them.
- The macOS flasher is a signed, notarised app.

1.0 (2026-08-19)
- Initial release.

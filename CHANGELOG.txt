2.1 (2026-09-02)
- Sequencer notes recorded below the initial octave now keep their MIDI notes after a power cycle.
- A tie at the end of a loop now holds its note to the next step instead of cutting it short.
- The clock divider’s gate is now half the step at every division.
- While the strip is held in record, its LEDs show which side a rest or a tie will go in on, and hold it for a moment after the release.

2.0 (2026-08-31)
- Step sequencer: 64 steps, recorded and played from the preset pads.
- External clock divider: the arpeggiator rate knob now divides a patched clock from /1 to /8.
- Preset knobs can now be assigned individually.
- Preset voltages are now set by holding a pad and turning the knob above it.
- Alternate tunings now accept Scala keyboard maps, so a scale can have any number of degrees and repeat at something other than the octave.
- Leaving the arpeggiator’s latch no longer stops notes still held by hand.

1.2 (2026-08-25)
- Improved pressure-based portamento behavior.
- Held-key state is cleared on the first boot after flashing, so a stuck gate no longer survives an update.

1.1 (2026-08-21)
- trn and remote enable stay factory when nothing has taken them.
- The macOS flasher is a signed, notarised app.

1.0 (2026-08-19)
- Initial release.
